    How the safety score works. Each route is checked edge by edge against OpenStreetMap. Road crossings are weighted by the size of the road crossed and whether there is a traffic-light crossing, a marked crossing, or nothing. Overhead bridges and underpasses count as protected. Stretches walked along a carriageway or through car parks add smaller penalties. Crossings inside a School Zone or Silver Zone carry 25% less risk because of the lower speed limits and traffic calming there. Score = 100 × e−risk/30. “Sheltered” is the share of the walk under an LTA covered linkway or sheltered overhead bridge.

    Pilot limits. OpenStreetMap does not record every zebra crossing or footpath in Singapore, so crossing types are cross-checked against LTA's own road-crossing records; a crossing shown as “no crossing facility recorded” has neither. Walk the route with your child before relying on it.
